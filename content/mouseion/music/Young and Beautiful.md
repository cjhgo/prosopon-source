## 专辑简介

"Young and Beautiful" is a song by American singer Lana Del Rey for the 2013 film adaptation of The Great Gatsby. It is set to be released on April 23 as the first single for the soundtrack with the video being released a day before. A couple months before the release date, she had sung part of the song for a radio station, which included the lyrics "will you still love me when I'm no longer young and beautiful?". Musically, the song is a piano ballad.
